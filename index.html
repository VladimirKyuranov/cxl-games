Questions

Before you register.

Is this really free?

Yes. All five worlds are free to play and no purchase is required anywhere — the faucet, quests, campaign and everyday earn paths are open to free accounts. Premium exists if you want to speed things up, but nothing on this page needs it.

Do I have to play all five games?

No. Register wherever you like — each account stands on its own. The reason to start with Lightning Quest is that its cleared campaign areas power the Linked Miner in Lightning Miner, so your progress there keeps paying off in a second world.

What exactly is the Linked Miner?

It's a miner Lightning Miner gives you for connecting your Lightning Quest account. It scales with your campaign progress at +1% mining power for every area you clear, and it sits alongside the miners you place on your own racks.

How does playing one game help another?

Three ways. Quest progress lifts your Linked Miner's power in Lightning Miner. Value moves between economies — Lightning Miner wallets support Lightning Quest transfers, and TRX Miner lists Lightning Quest and Quest Coins among its payout paths. And one CXL Premium membership covers every properly linked world at once.

How do withdrawals work?

It varies by game. Lightning Quest pays out in BTC Lightning, BTC, LTC, SOL, USDC, DOGE, ETH or PayPal through a direct wallet, FaucetPay or PayPal. Lightning Miner supports FaucetPay, BTC Lightning, direct Bitcoin, CXL Wallet and Lightning Quest transfers where available. TRX Miner offers instant FaucetPay TRX withdrawals when eligible. Minimums, fees and review requirements are always shown inside each game.

Do I need a CXL Games account?

Not to play — but linking is where the network benefits live: one shared Premium clock, 2% cashback on eligible purchases, 2× daily Discord rewards on linked worlds, and automatic syncing when you add another game later.

Can I run more than one account?

No. Every one of these worlds allows one account per person, and using VPNs, proxies or extra accounts to farm rewards, referrals or withdrawals leads to a ban. Play one honest account per world.
